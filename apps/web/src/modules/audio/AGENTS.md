# Audio (web)

- Audio outputs: `modules/audio/output-switch.tsx` (`OutputSwitch`) is the configured outputs as one Hairline `ButtonGroup` (the fill glides to the active one, `aria-pressed`), backed by `audio.setOutput({ label })`; it sits in the small widget and in the Media panel. The Deck `AudioControlTile` opens a small Dialog with the same output switch, a volume slider and mute; microphone stays a separate tile. Loading/disconnected audio disables the tile and dialog controls. Media and the Deck dialog use volume and mute controls (the volume slider keeps a draft while dragging and writes once on release or key up (`Slider`'s `onValueCommit`), like the Luci slider: one `setVolume` per drag, so parallel `wpctl` calls cannot land out of order). In `modules/audio/widget.tsx`, `small` places the live volume `NumberRoll` in the header, active output name + `OutputSwitch` on the bottom row, and a stretched `/desk/system` link behind the switch; `medium` and `wide` stay on `AudioPanel`. The vendored ButtonGroup was adapted: primary fill for the selected pill (the accent had too little contrast), md segments at `h-touch`, and its spacing/radius literals mapped to tokens.
