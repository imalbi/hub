# Notes (web)

- References store a source id, record id and title snapshot, never an href. `AboutLink` resolves the href through the enabled source supplied by the desk shell and retains the snapshot if it is absent; both Oggi and the widget render that caption.

- Panoramica widget (`widget.tsx`, `days.ts`, step 67 Part C1): `medium` renders `<Kbd>N</Kbd>` + `Aggiungi` (`normal-case` to override `Panel`'s `text-caps` wrapper) and a bottom-anchored row of up to 3 `nearestNotes` with `noteDayLabel` (`Oggi`, `Domani`, or short `gio 19`); `large` renders the 3-column day board (`Oggi`, `Domani`, `Più avanti`) allocated chronologically by `groupBoardNotes` (`LARGE_COLUMN_MAX = 3`, `LARGE_NOTES_MAX = 6`) with `altri N post-it` at the foot and date captions only under `Più avanti`. Compact cards clamp text to 2 lines and reveal `Stacca` on hover/focus (`bg-surface border`). `wide` and `full` keep the existing 3-column `NoteCard` grid, inline editing, and "In arrivo" dashed cards (`notes.visual.spec.ts` baselines unchanged).

- Post-its (step 38, `modules/notes/`): the desk shell mounts `NoteCapture` (a `useModal` dialog, not `Dialog`, so the card has no title row) and `NoteUndoToast`, and binds plain `n` (skipped in fields, with modifiers, and while any `dialog[open]` is on screen); `store.ts` is the small external store for "capture open" and the last removed post-it. Oggi shows the post-its through `ModuleUI.todayColumn` (`notes/today.tsx`, `NotesColumn({ day, variant, max })`, step 66): a count in Medio, a titled column in Largo, a titled row of two cards in Grande (two rows overflowed the cell with four lines and weather, step 64 review), a bare stack per day in Esteso, where tomorrow's ends with its count and Aggiungi (`openCapture('tomorrow')` preselects Domani). Cards show one text line with the origin muted after it, and the reference caption on that same line (a second line overflowed Largo with two cards, step 71 review), cut by `max` with "e altre N": never scrolled. The widget is offered in Panoramica but not in the default board. The day picker is the browser's own calendar (a hidden `input[type=date]` opened with `showPicker`), not a drawn one. `notes.visual.spec.ts` swaps the `notes.onOpen` subscription data by request id (the client batches requests into arrays).
